# Tokyo --- Accommodation Base

## Recommendation

**Best area: Shimbashi / Uchisaiwaicho / Daimon / Hamamatsucho**

For this itinerary, search for accommodation within roughly **5--10
minutes on foot of Shimbashi, Daimon, or Hamamatsucho**.

### Priority

1.  **Shimbashi / Uchisaiwaicho** --- best overall
2.  **Daimon / Hamamatsucho** --- almost as good; compare apartment
    quality/price
3.  **Yurakucho / Ginza** --- excellent but likely more expensive
4.  **Tokyo Station / Yaesu** --- excellent transport, but little reason
    to pay a premium

## Why this area

The saved places are spread across:

-   **Ginza:** Daiso, MUJI, Ginza Itoya, Uniqlo
-   **Shinjuku:** Golden-Gai, Metropolitan Government Building, Shinjuku
    Gyoen
-   **Shibuya / Daikanyama:** Shibuya Sky, SHIBUYA TSUTAYA, DAIKANYAMA
    T-SITE, Starbucks Reserve Roastery
-   **Ueno / Akihabara:** Ameyoko, Akihabara Electric Town, GiGO, KIELO
    COFFEE
-   **Minato / waterfront:** Hamarikyu Gardens, Prince Shiba Park,
    Rainbow Bridge, Tokyo Rainbow Sewerage Museum
-   **Other:** Nezu Museum, Happo-en, Institute for Nature Study,
    Kyu-Furukawa Gardens, etc.

The itinerary therefore covers both **west Tokyo and east/central
Tokyo**. Staying in Shinjuku or Shibuya would make the western side
convenient but create more repeated trips east.

## Transport advantages

### From Shimbashi

-   **Ginza:** very close / walkable
-   **Shibuya:** direct JR Yamanote or Ginza Line
-   **Shinjuku:** direct JR Yamanote
-   **Tokyo / Akihabara / Ueno:** direct JR Yamanote
-   **Odaiba / Rainbow Bridge:** direct **Yurikamome**
-   **Hamarikyu:** very close
-   **Shiba Park:** nearby

### Nearby Daimon / Hamamatsucho

Adds:

-   JR Yamanote / Keihin-Tohoku
-   Toei Asakusa Line
-   Toei Oedo Line
-   Tokyo Monorail
-   Easy access back to Shimbashi/Yurikamome

## Why not stay elsewhere?

### Shinjuku

Good for the Shinjuku cluster, but less convenient for the repeated
Ginza, Akihabara/Ueno and waterfront trips.

### Shibuya

Excellent for Shibuya/Daikanyama, but relatively inconvenient for Ginza,
Ueno/Akihabara and Odaiba.

### Ueno

Excellent for the eastern cluster, but less balanced for
Shibuya/Shinjuku.

### Odaiba

Convenient for the waterfront only; poor overall base.

## Practical decision rule

Do **not** choose an apartment based on a 1--2 minute difference in
train time.

Within the recommended area, prioritize:

1.  **≤10 min walk to station**
2.  Good apartment / price
3.  Quiet enough for sleeping
4.  Convenient supermarket/convenience store nearby
5.  Then optimize the exact station

**Best search area:**
`Shimbashi → Uchisaiwaicho → Daimon → Hamamatsucho`
