## If we don't have a Chinese SIM

We can ask Air China or airport staff to contact the hotel for us.

**Show this in Chinese:**

> 我们有国航确认的中转酒店预订，但是没有中国手机卡。可以帮我们联系酒店安排接机吗？
>
> Wǒmen yǒu Guóháng quèrèn de zhōngzhuǎn jiǔdiàn yùdìng, dànshì méiyǒu Zhōngguó shǒujī kǎ. Kěyǐ bāng wǒmen liánxì jiǔdiàn ānpái jiējī ma?

**Meaning:** We have a confirmed Air China transit hotel booking, but we don't have a Chinese SIM. Could you help us contact the hotel to arrange airport pickup?

Show the hotel confirmation and booking number as well.

## If arriving at T2

The Air China confirmation says **T2 passengers do not need to go to the Air China Transit Service Counter**.

Contact the hotel directly for pickup. If we cannot make the call, ask airport/Air China staff to contact the hotel.

## If arriving at T3

1. Follow **Arrivals** signs and leave the baggage-claim area.
2. Go to **T3-C, Level 2, Exit B**.
3. The **Air China Transit Service Counter** is opposite Exit B.
4. Staff can confirm the booking and contact the hotel for pickup.

If the counter is closed, contact the hotel or Air China (`95583`).

## Airport help

If we need help, go to an airport information/service desk and show the hotel confirmation.
