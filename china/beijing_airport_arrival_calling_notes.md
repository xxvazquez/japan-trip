## Beijing Capital Airport (PEK) --- useful arrival information

### Airport help

Beijing Capital Airport has **Airport Service Ambassadors / information
staff** who can help passengers with airport procedures, directions and
airport services.

Airport information hotline:

**010-96158 --- 24 hours**

Useful information-counter locations include:

-   **T2:** Level 2, opposite International Gate 6
-   **T3:** T3-C Level 2, Area B arrivals/reception area
-   **T3:** T3-C Level 2, international baggage hall near carousel 39

If you cannot call the hotel yourself, go to an information/service
counter and show the hotel confirmation and phone number.

## Air China transit-hotel procedure

### Arriving at Terminal 2

Your booking confirmation says passengers arriving at **T2 do not need
to go to the Air China Transit Service Counter**.

Contact the hotel for pickup directly. If you cannot make the call
yourself, ask Air China or airport staff to help you contact the hotel.

### Arriving at Terminal 3

For Air China standard transit accommodation:

1.  Follow **Arrivals** signs.
2.  Leave the baggage-claim area.
3.  Go to **T3-C, Level 2, Exit B**.
4.  Look for the **Air China Transit Service Counter opposite Exit B**.
5.  Staff can confirm the booking and coordinate the hotel pickup.

Air China's current transit-accommodation information also confirms that
T3 passengers can go directly to the Air China Transit Service Counter
for hotel-pickup coordination.

If the counter is closed, contact the hotel directly.

## Getting a SIM / internet at PEK if needed

Beijing Capital Airport currently lists **SIM-card and portable Wi-Fi
service points in both T2 and T3**.

Useful locations include:

-   **T2:** Level 1 international-arrivals baggage-claim hall, near
    carousel 3 --- listed hours 07:00--19:00
-   **T2:** Level 2 public area near International Departures Gate 4 ---
    portable Wi-Fi service listed as 24 hours
-   **T3:** T3-C Level 2 international baggage-claim hall, near carousel
    38 --- listed as 24 hours
-   **T3:** Level 4 public area, near check-in island D --- portable
    Wi-Fi service listed as 24 hours

A SIM is therefore an option after arrival, but **it should not be
necessary just to arrange the transit-hotel pickup**.
